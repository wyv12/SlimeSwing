# Slime Swing Jank Build

A separate, deliberately slapdash arena prototype. It has five fixed-screen rooms, grappling knots, and sentries, with occasional reversed movement, a wobbly tendril, and suspiciously bad turret aim. The glitches are intentional; the project still opens and plays.

## Controls

- **W / A / S / D** or **Arrow keys** — move and pump your swing
- **Mouse** — aim at a glowing grapple knot
- **Hold left mouse button** — attach the tendril
- **Release left mouse button** — let go
- **Escape** — pause or resume
- **Grapple-ram a turret at speed** — destroy it and bounce clear

Destroy every turret in a room to advance. Open this folder as its own Unity project and press Play in the default scene.
