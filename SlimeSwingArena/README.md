# Slime Swing Arena

A five-level, fixed-screen top-down arena game. The slime moves around each room, swings from glowing knots, and can grapple-ram sentries to destroy them. Ordinary contact and sentry bolts hurt. Clear a room by destroying every turret; the next arena fades in automatically. The game starts directly in the first room.

## Controls

- **W / A / S / D** or **Arrow keys** — move and pump your swing
- **Mouse** — aim at a glowing grapple knot
- **Hold left mouse button** — attach the tendril
- **Release left mouse button** — let go
- **Escape** — pause or resume
- **Grapple-ram a turret at speed** — destroy it and bounce clear

Open the project in Unity Hub and press Play in the default scene.
